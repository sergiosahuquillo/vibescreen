Display protocol
----------------

turing-smart-screen-python - GPL-3.0
https://github.com/mathoudebine/turing-smart-screen-python
The serial protocol of the displays comes from the reverse engineering work of
this project, and its logic is used to generate the test vectors of the
protocol encoder.
